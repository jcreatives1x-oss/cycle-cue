**Description:**

Cycle Cue is a cycle companion built for partners.

Enter when her last period started and Cycle Cue shows where she is in her month, what her body is likely going through, and how you can show up for her that day.

TODAY AT A GLANCE

• A plain-language note on what her hormones are likely doing
• What helps, what to skip, and something kind to say

GET HER SOMETHING
• Phase-specific ideas, from a heating pad to her comfort snacks
• One tap opens Instacart, DoorDash or Amazon with the search ready

HEADS-UP REMINDERS
• Two days before her PMS days
• The day her period is due
• The start of her fertile window, if you're trying to conceive

PLAN AHEAD
• Expected dates for the next three cycles
• A comfort list to save what helps her, in her own words

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no tracking, no ads. Everything stays on your phone.

Cycle Cue gives general information, not medical advice. Predictions are estimates and are not reliable as contraception.

**App Privacy (nutrition label):** Data Not Collected.

**Age rating questionnaire:** answer "Infrequent/Mild" for Medical/Treatment Information and "None" for everything else. That should give a 12+ rating.

**Notes for the App Review team:**
Cycle Cue works without sign-in. Open the app to see sample dates, then change "Last period started" under Settings to see the guidance update. Reminders are scheduled locally after you allow notifications. The store buttons open the public Instacart, DoorDash and Amazon search pages. The app does not process purchases.
