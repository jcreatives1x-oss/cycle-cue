Privacy policy

Cycle Cue · Effective October 9, 2026

Summary

Cycle Cue does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

You may enter a period start date, cycle length, period length, reminder choices and free-text notes in the comfort list. This information is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it.

No accounts, analytics or advertising

Cycle Cue has no sign-in, no analytics or tracking tools, no advertising, and no third-party software development kits that collect data.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules notifications locally on your device using the operating system's notification service. No server is involved.

Links to other services

The "Get her something" section contains links to third-party stores such as Instacart, DoorDash and Amazon. Tapping a link opens that service's app or website with a search term filled in. The only information passed is the search term shown, such as "heating pad". Your cycle data is never included. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Children

Cycle Cue is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Medical disclaimer

Cycle Cue provides general information and is not medical advice. Predictions are estimates and must not be used as contraception.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
